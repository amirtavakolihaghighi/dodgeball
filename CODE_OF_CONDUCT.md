# Code of conduct

This project is about a game people play together in the same room, and the same spirit
applies here.

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** People report bugs and suggest ideas because they care.
- **Discuss ideas, not people.** Disagree with a change; don't put down the person who made it.
- **No harassment of any kind**: no insults, slurs, threats, unwanted attention, or posting
  anyone's private information (that includes names and addresses from session logs).
- **Keep it on topic** and keep it welcoming for newcomers.

If something here makes you uncomfortable, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
comment or issue, or reach the maintainer privately through the **Security** tab's report form
(only the maintainer sees it). Reports are kept confidential. Comments, issues or contributions that break
these rules may be edited or removed, and repeated or serious cases lead to a ban from the
project.
