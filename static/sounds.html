Every sound

Each button plays what the game plays at that moment, with the same volumes as your settings in the game. Most sounds have several recordings and a little pitch change each time, so press a button a few times. “From the left”, “From the right” and “Far away” show how a sound changes with where it happens. Click any button to start the sound.
